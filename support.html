KOKO Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: studio4any@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

KOKO says my iPhone isn't supported

KOKO needs iOS 26 and an iPhone 15 Pro, 15 Pro Max, or iPhone 16 or later, with Apple Intelligence turned on and downloaded.

Korean speech recognition isn't available

Recognition runs on your iPhone. Add Korean under Settings, General, Keyboard, Dictation, then try again.

The voice sounds robotic

Download an Enhanced or Premium Korean voice in Settings, Accessibility, Read & Speak, Voices.

My scheduled call didn't ring

Allow notifications for KOKO in Settings, and check that a Focus isn't silencing them.

Cancel or restore a subscription

Manage it in Settings, your name, Subscriptions. To restore, open the subscription screen in KOKO and tap Restore purchases.
