KOKO Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

KOKO does not collect your personal data. There is no KOKO account and no KOKO server.

What stays on your iPhone

Your microphone audio, transcripts, call recordings, reports, saved phrases and the facts KOKO remembers are processed and stored on your device. Speech recognition, replies and voices use Apple's on-device features. Recordings are excluded from device backups. Other app data may be included in your own iCloud or computer backup, depending on your settings.

Permissions

KOKO uses the microphone and speech recognition to hold calls, and notifications to ring you at times you choose. You can turn these off in Settings.

Subscription

Apple handles payment. KOKO checks your subscription status through the App Store and never sees your payment details. Apple may provide us with aggregated sales reports.

Reports from Apple

If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers, or send feedback through TestFlight, Apple may give us crash reports or the feedback you sent. This does not include your calls, transcripts or recordings unless you attach them yourself.

Deleting your data

Delete any memory, phrase or call in the app, or use You, then Delete all my data. Deleting the app removes its data from your iPhone.

Children

KOKO does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
